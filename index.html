The real problem

Software sorts. People decide.

Many employers run applications through an applicant tracking system (ATS) and AI ranking tools that search resumes for the words in the job posting. If your resume describes your work in different words, your real experience can be hard to spot — even when you're qualified.

We help you say what you really did, in the words the job uses. A person still makes the call.

Example · same experience, clearer words
BeforeDid inventory stuff and drove the forklift.
AfterRan daily cycle counts and moved pallets by forklift, using an RF scanner for inventory control.

Only true details go in. If you didn't do it, we don't add it. Match scores are AI estimates, not an official ATS score.
